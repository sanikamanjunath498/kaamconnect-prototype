# KaamConnect — Voice-First Job Matching & Skill Passport

**Bol kar kaam paayein** — A mobile-first prototype designed to help skilled workers discover local jobs via voice prompts, check employer trustworthiness and fair daily wages, showcase verified work proof, and respond to urgent or team-hiring alerts.

## ✨ Prototype Highlights
- **Voice Onboarding (`Chat` tab):** Simulates 1-tap voice capture, intent extraction (skill, area, availability), a multi-language display toggle (`English`, `हिन्दी`, `ಕನ್ನಡ`), and direct navigation to matching jobs.
- **Distance-Sorted Job Matches (`Jobs` tab):** Displays nearby listings with daily pay, fair-rate vs. below-market badges, and employer verification previews.
- **Employer Trust & Scam Check:** Shows employer ratings, job history, market wage comparison, a 3-point safety check, warnings on unverified accounts, and **Accept job** / **Report** flows.
- **Skill Passport (`Passport` tab):** Features a worker profile, AI/peer-verified skill tags, tappable work photo inspection cards, a scannable-style QR preview, share options, and a data-ownership notice.
- **Alerts & Team Hiring (`Alerts` tab):** Includes an urgent nearby dispatch card (**Accept / Skip**) and a combined multi-worker crew card (**Mason + Helper + Painter**).

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **HTML5** (single-file mobile frame prototype)
- **Tailwind CSS** (via CDN)
- **Vanilla JavaScript**
- **Google Fonts** (`Poppins`)
